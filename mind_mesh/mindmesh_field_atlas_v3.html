Field guide

The epistemic contract

Every card has an objective best answer grounded in cited research. It also exposes a boundary condition because an average effect is not a diagnosis of a person, relationship, team, or culture.

Confidence before feedback

Choose how likely your answer is to be correct before locking it. The report separately shows accuracy, average confidence, confidence gap, expected calibration error, and Brier loss.

Hints have a cost

Three progressively revealing margin notes are available. In scored modes, each note lowers the positive point multiplier. A wrong high-confidence answer also loses more points than a tentative miss.

What the report means

Chapter results describe performance on the sampled knowledge cards only. They do not measure your empathy, moral character, leadership quality, or real-world social competence.

Keyboard map

  • 1–4 select an answer.
  • Arrow keys move within the answer radio group.
  • H reveals a hint.
  • Left/right changes confidence.
  • Enter locks or continues.
  • Escape closes dialogs.

Three play formats

Solo fieldwork focuses on calibration. Table relay rotates turns among local teams. Open notebook removes point pressure for study and discussion.
