Why this game exists

An antidote to confident ignorance

The Human Edge is a social game and private practice ground for ideas that change how you decide, learn, persuade, coordinate, invest, and protect yourself.

No hot takes. No personality tests. No philosophical riddles disguised as facts. Every card aims at a checkable answer, explicit assumptions, useful mechanism, and evidence trail.

For group play, read only the question. A team may ask for up to three hints, each reducing the card’s value by one point. Reveal the answer, then reward the mechanism—not merely the keyword. In solo play, call your confidence first and grade yourself honestly.
